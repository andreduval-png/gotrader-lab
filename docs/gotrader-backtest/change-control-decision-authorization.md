## Authorized Scope

Inventory the proposed architecture change-control policy and existing frozen
governance contracts. Produce a machine-readable and human-readable decision
that binds every future slice to an exact clean parent, explicit authorization,
separate implementation and acceptance commits, complete gate evidence,
failure preservation, bounded rollback, immutable evidence retention, and
named human review boundaries.

The decision may adopt a process only. It grants no feature, runtime, readiness,
broker, production, Paper Demo, or execution authority.

## Prohibited Scope

- No BT3A or later-phase authorization or implementation.
- No runtime adoption, automatic startup, external contact, or readiness change.
- No strategy execution, simulation promotion, parameter search, statistics
  promotion, risk allocation, portfolio action, Paper Demo, broker mutation,
  trade intent, or execution.
- No deletion or rewriting of accepted evidence or honest failure records.
- No raw-candle commit.

Authority remains `none/none/none`.
