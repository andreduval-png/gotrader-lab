# G1.1 BT2 Parity

Accepted I2 adapters now construct canonical G1.1 geometry before creating a BT2
request. The request retains its original entry zone and also carries the exact
canonical identity, intended entry midpoint, structural stop, and native target.

The compact boundary assertion fails if canonical intended entry, stop, or target
diverges from the BT2 request. ICT 2022 declares its accepted primary external
draw policy. Power of Three retains its profile objective category. Neither
adapter searches for a farther target.

BT2 exclusively owns actual fill, touch-vs-fill, spread, slippage, commission,
same-bar stop/target ordering, net R:R, and realized R. G1.1 does not simulate or
claim those effects.
