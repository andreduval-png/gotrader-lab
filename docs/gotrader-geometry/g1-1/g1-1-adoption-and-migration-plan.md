# G1.1 Adoption and Migration Plan

## Implemented

1. Canonical immutable contract and identity.
2. Entry lifecycle and no-chase policy.
3. Causal target candidates and explicit fallback policy.
4. Signed theoretical R:R and actionability.
5. Current Opportunity shadow geometry and lossless activation projection.
6. Operator Console canonical-first rendering and removal of implied-entry recovery.
7. Accepted I2-to-BT2 canonical parity.

## Selected first strategy adoption

Order Block Retracement is first because its explicit nearest directional draw
created the visible too-close target class. Its native levels are now preserved
as research geometry in the shared projection path. A follow-up strategy-owned
policy must distinguish its primary structural draw from merely nearest
liquidity before the detector itself is declared fully migrated.

## Remaining staged migrations

Legacy advisor completion, individual IFVG/Silver Bullet/Turtle Soup/CISD/CMD/
OSOK/Bread & Butter detectors, and the external research MCP compatibility path
must produce or require the canonical object directly. They are fail-closed or
research-only at adopted UI boundaries, but their internal primitive producers
remain documented migration limitations.

Paper Demo authority is unchanged. Any future gateway adoption must require
`actionable === true`, current identity, and all existing readiness/runbook gates.
