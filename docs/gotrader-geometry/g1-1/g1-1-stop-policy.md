# G1.1 Stop Policy

The strategy owns the native structural invalidation. G1.1 records its model,
price, source fact, owner timeframe, and whether it is structural. The shared
service validates direction but cannot tighten a stop to manufacture R:R.

Any buffer must name a versioned policy. A later symbol-normalization adoption
must use canonical digits, point size, and tick/step semantics; no generic G1.1
fallback mixes futures ticks, CFD points, pips, or raw units. Legacy generic stop
completion remains a documented migration limitation and cannot produce adopted
canonical actionable geometry without a strategy policy.
