# I0 Concept Versus Strategy Taxonomy

## Taxonomy

| Layer | May do | Must not do | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primitive fact | Describe one causal market fact | Emit a trade plan or claim edge | swing, FVG, sweep, displacement |
| Derived context | Combine facts into market state | Own eligibility or native geometry | dealing range, PD hierarchy, C1 narrative, S1 SMT |
| Recognition | Name a forming pattern | Create evidence or promotion status | universal recognition, generic scalp, PD-array setup |
| Confluence model | Score/support a registered setup | Emit a standalone opportunity unless separately specified | Grinch profiles |
| Strategy model | Emit deterministic candidate and native geometry | Own simulated fills or execution | IFVG, Silver Bullet, LRS |
| Canonical adapter | Translate a strategy candidate losslessly | Invent direction, levels, or blockers | BT3 adapters |
| BT2 simulator | Resolve fills, costs, ambiguity, expiry, outcomes | Alter strategy semantics or parameters | BT2 ledger/simulator |
| Evidence/readiness | Bind reports to exact identities | Convert historical/unmatched evidence into current proof | validation chain, RQ, readiness |
| Presentation | Render source-of-truth records | Recompute or embellish results | Results, Current Read, Advisor |

## Non-Negotiable Distinctions

- Premium/discount is context; OTE is a setup sequence that may consume it.
- An order block is a primitive/derived fact; Bread and Butter is a strategy that consumes order-block facts.
- SMT is context unless a separately specified SMT strategy is created. Missing optional SMT is not a blocker;
  opposing SMT may be a blocker according to the frozen strategy policy.
- C1/C1.1 narrative explains structure and setup-relative flow. It does not override detector eligibility.
- A Grinch model is confluence-only under the accepted contract.
- “Scalp” and “swing” describe intended holding/management horizons; they are not strategy identities.
- A trade outcome belongs to BT2 or an immutable forward-evidence ledger, not to a detector or UI.
- Historical evidence with a different strategy/profile/parameter/source/run identity must be labeled historical,
  never current validation.

## Duplicate Resolution Policy

1. Select the V2 canonical fact contract as future owner.
2. Freeze the legacy output and build parity fixtures.
3. Add a compatibility adapter rather than changing every consumer at once.
4. Prove timestamp, geometry, blocker, and identity parity.
5. Migrate one consumer in an authorized slice.
6. Deprecate the duplicate only after all consumers and evidence manifests point to the canonical owner.

No mass rewrite is authorized by I0.
