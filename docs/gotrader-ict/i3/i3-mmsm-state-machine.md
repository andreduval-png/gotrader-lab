# MMSM State Machine

MMSM uses the same causal phases and terminal states as MMBM. Direction-specific semantics are mirrored:

- discount becomes premium;
- sell-side engineering becomes buy-side engineering;
- bullish transition/displacement become bearish;
- bullish PD arrays become bearish;
- buyside objective becomes sellside objective;
- long signed geometry becomes short signed geometry.

The symmetry fixture mirrors prices around the range equilibrium and requires equal phase progression, risk distance, reward distance, theoretical R:R, geometry status, and authority. Strategy and geometry identities remain distinct.
