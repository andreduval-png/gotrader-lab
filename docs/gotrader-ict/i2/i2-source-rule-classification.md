# I2 Source Rule Classification

I2 consumes I1 facts and never creates strategy-specific swing, liquidity, FVG, displacement, MSS, range, or clock detectors. Rules are classified in `ictI2SourcePackets.ts` as `SOURCE_DEFINED`, `CANONICAL_GOTRADER_RULE`, `RESEARCH_PARAMETER`, or `UNRESOLVED`.

ICT 2022 is research-resolved around ordered objective, opposite-side raid, displacement, MSS, FVG, retrace, structural stop, and external draw. PO3 is research-resolved around canonical range, range-side consumption, displacement/MSS distribution, and structural objective. Numeric expiry, entry mode, and optional session/profile choices are preregistered research parameters.

Judas Swing is source-blocked. Its exact session window, opening reference, and reversal-confirmation combination are material and unresolved. Pattern evidence cannot bypass this gate. SMT is optional S1 context for all base profiles; no model implements SMT.

All components that define causal identity are mandatory and inseparable from their preceding facts. Profile choices are optional; dependent entry geometry cannot exist without the full mandatory sequence.
